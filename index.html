HYDROPOWER INFRASTRUCTURE

Run-of-River Baselines: Decoupling Compute From Fossil Grids.

Traditional cloud inference incurs severe ecological debt through thermal power backing. Northern Mindanao inverted this model by colocating high-throughput TPU and GPU clusters directly adjacent to the 138kV sub-transmission switchyards of the Agus IV and Pulangi IV hydroelectric plants.

Natural river discharge fluctuations are stabilized via the regional SCADA frequency governor, enabling dynamic compute workloads to modulate clock frequencies with turbine intake velocity. Convective water loops maintain an exceptional Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) of 1.06 without mechanical refrigerants.
