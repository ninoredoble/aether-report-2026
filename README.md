# Aether Report 2026

> **The 2026 Northern Mindanao AI & Autonomous Systems Impact Report**  
> An editorial scrollytelling publication and empirical study examining edge artificial intelligence, distributed renewable compute topologies, and ecological telemetry systems across Region X.

---

## 🌐 Overview & Executive Thesis

In 2026, artificial intelligence in the Philippine archipelago made a decisive transition from centralized overseas server dependencies to decentralized regional edge meshes. 

**Aether Report 2026** documents Northern Mindanao's emergence as an autonomous compute haven—coupling clean **Agus-Pulangi run-of-river hydropower** directly to low-latency edge inference nodes across Cagayan de Oro, Tagoloan, and Mount Kitanglad.

---

## 📊 Key Findings & Macro Indicators

- **18.4 PFLOPS Clean Compute Capacity**: Aggregate regional edge capacity powered with zero-carbon attribution directly from Mindanao's hydropower cascades.
- **4.2ms Median Edge Inference (p99 8.1ms)**: Real-time responsiveness across 14 municipal telemetry networks.
- **128,000 Active Mesh Sensors**: Continuous environmental monitoring spanning soil moisture, marine salinity in Macajalar Bay, and sub-transmission grid load flows.
- **91.4% Carbon Intensity Reduction**: Relative to traditional fossil-backed cloud training pipelines.

---

## 🛠️ Interactive Engineering Features

1. **Ambient Fluid Shader Canvas**: Real-time canvas gradient simulation evoking Northern Mindanao's river cascades and mist canopies.
2. **Reactive Telemetry SVG Chart**: Dynamic visualization tracking clean hydropower baseload vs. compute density growth from 2024 to 2026.
3. **In-Context AI Agent (Aether Auditor)**: An embedded micro-agent capable of parsing and responding to natural language queries regarding regional compute architecture, flood alert windows, and carbon offsets.
